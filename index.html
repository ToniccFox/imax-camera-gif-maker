Enable the "Save GIF" menu toggle in VRChat, then place your VRChat camera inside of the black orb. Take a photo, then use the "Save Next Frame" button to view the next frame, and take another photo of the next frame. Continue until you have a photo of each of the 16 frames. Find your photos/frames in Pictures\VRChat (by default), then drag and drop them all here. Files are sorted by name, so the VRChat camera's timestamps already put them in order.
